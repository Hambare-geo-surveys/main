HAMBARE GEOSURVEYS — PROJECT BRIEF VIDEO TRANSCRIPT

The form is divided into three short stages.

1. Project basics
Choose the survey service that most closely matches the decision you need to make. Select “Not sure yet” when the correct service is unclear. Enter the project location, project stage and approximate site size.

2. Outputs and logistics
Add the output you expect, any plans or records already available, your preferred timing and site-access information.

3. Purpose and contact
Explain what the survey will support. Add your name and WhatsApp number.

Select “Prepare WhatsApp brief.” Review the generated message, open WhatsApp, and attach any survey plans, title documents, drawings or site photographs before sending.
